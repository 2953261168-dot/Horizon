Score substantive global news from 0 to 10 by public impact, novelty, timeliness, quality of evidence and relevance across regions. Give major policy decisions, verified crises, international agreements and significant public health findings high scores. Distinguish confirmed facts, attributed statements and uncertainty. Penalize sensationalism, unsupported claims and routine promotion. A government or company source establishes its announcement, not that its claims are objectively true. Never inflate scores merely because a source is famous. Do not require technology relevance.
