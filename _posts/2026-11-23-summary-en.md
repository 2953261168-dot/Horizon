### [ECB Expands Acceptance of DLT-Based Assets as Eligible Eurosystem Collateral](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//press/pr/date/2026/html/ecb.pr261123~76b6f57b74.en.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The European Central Bank \(ECB\) has expanded the acceptance of assets issued on distributed ledger technology \(DLT\) platforms as eligible collateral for Eurosystem credit operations. This decision supports innovation and technological progress in European financial markets, impacting students of accounting and finance who may see future changes in accounting and auditing standards.

rss · 欧洲央行 — 新闻发布 · Nov 23, 10:00

**「Understanding DLT-Based Assets and Eurosystem Collateral」** Distributed Ledger Technology \(DLT\) is a digital technology that allows the recording of transactions across many computers so that the recorded data cannot be altered retroactively without the alteration of all subsequent blocks and the consensus of the network. The European Central Bank \(ECB\) has recently expanded the acceptance of DLT-based assets as eligible collateral for Eurosystem credit operations. This means that assets issued on DLT platforms licensed under the EU DLT Pilot Regime Regulation and made available on the TARGET2-Securities platform can now be used as collateral for credit operations. The TARGET2-Securities platform is a Eurosystem platform for securities settlement in central bank money, offering centralised delivery-versus-payment \(DvP\) settlement across several European securities markets. This decision by the ECB is part of a roadmap to explore the eligibility and on-chain mobilisation of assets issued and settled entirely on DLT networks, supporting innovation and technological progress in European financial markets.

**「Impact on Financial Markets and Accounting Practices」** The expansion of DLT-based assets as eligible Eurosystem collateral by the European Central Bank signifies a shift towards embracing new technologies in the financial sector. This move could lead to a reevaluation of accounting and auditing standards, as financial institutions may need to adapt their practices to accommodate these new types of assets. It raises the question of how the integration of DLT will affect the transparency and accuracy of financial reporting, and what new challenges it may pose for auditors.
