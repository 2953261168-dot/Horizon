This feature is designed to optimize the delivery of large files, reducing both network and disk usage. It works by allowing clients to request only a portion of a file, rather than the entire file, which is particularly useful for video players, download managers, and package managers. In the past, Cloudflare&\#x27;s caching mechanism for range requests could lead to inefficiencies, such as unnecessary data transfer and increased disk operations. The new implementation, based on a Pingora caching server, addresses these issues by storing files in fixed-size chunks and fetching only the required bytes from the origin. This change is now available as a self-service feature for all Cloudflare users, eliminating the need for technical tuning or support. For users, this means improved efficiency and potentially lower costs for bandwidth and storage.

**「Impact on Ordinary Life」** Origin Range Requests by Cloudflare could lead to faster and more efficient delivery of large files, reducing unnecessary data usage. **「Understanding UUIDv7」** UUIDv7, or Version 7 of the Universally Unique Identifier standard, is designed to provide certain properties like monotonically increasing IDs, a helpful timestamp, and unguessable IDs. However, as discussed in a blog post by Alex Sci, these properties are not strictly held in certain implementations. The timestamp embedded in a UUIDv7 ID may not accurately represent the object&\#x27;s creation time, and the RFC 9562 allows for alterations to the timestamp field. This can lead to inconsistencies and potential issues in systems that rely on the accuracy of timestamps. Additionally, UUIDv7 IDs are not strictly monotonic, which means they may not always increase in a predictable order. Despite these challenges, UUIDv7 still offers the useful property of uniqueness and collision resistance, which is crucial for database operations and ensuring data integrity. It&\#x27;s important for developers to be aware of these nuances when using UUIDv7 in their systems.

**「Impact on Database Performance and UUID Usage」** The analysis of UUIDv7&\#x27;s properties reveals significant implications for database performance and the use of UUIDs in software development. The deviation from expected benefits, such as the lack of strict monotonicity and the potential for timestamp discrepancies, suggests that developers should be cautious when implementing UUIDv7. This caution is particularly important for those who rely on UUIDs for database performance optimizations, as the expected benefits of improved index locality and reduced read operations may not be realized. The discussion also highlights the importance of understanding the specific implementation details of UUIDv7, as different implementations may exhibit varying behaviors.
