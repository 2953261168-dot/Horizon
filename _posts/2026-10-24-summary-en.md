rss · BBC 教育 · Oct 23, 05:17

**「Understanding Alternative Communication Methods」** Alternative communication methods are essential tools for individuals who struggle to speak. One such method is Makaton, a form of Augmentative and Alternative Communication \(AAC\) that combines signs, symbols, and speech. Makaton is used to support communication, language development, and social interaction. Another method is eye gaze, where individuals use their eye movements to operate devices or communicate through visual cues. Electronic communication devices are also widely used, providing a means for non-verbal individuals to express themselves. Mitchell House Special School in Belfast has been recognized for its work in using these methods, emphasizing that communication is not just about speech and that every voice matters.

**「Impact」** The song &\#x27;My Voice&\#x27; serves as a powerful reminder that everyone&\#x27;s communication style is valuable and important, fostering empathy and understanding among young people.

### [OpenShell: A Secure Runtime for Autonomous AI Agents](https://github.com/NVIDIA/OpenShell) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenShell introduces a secure and private runtime environment for autonomous AI agents, ensuring they operate with restricted access to data and systems, crucial for AI and cybersecurity enthusiasts.

hackernews · niemandhier · Oct 22, 17:44 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50198675)

**「Understanding OpenShell」** OpenShell is an innovative project developed by NVIDIA, designed as a secure and private runtime environment for autonomous AI agents. It is built to provide a controlled environment where AI agents can perform tasks like reading files, installing packages, and calling APIs without compromising the user&\#x27;s data, secrets, or network. OpenShell achieves this by implementing kernel-level enforcement and formal verification, ensuring that each agent operates within a defined policy and that any changes to these policies are thoroughly reviewed. This approach is particularly relevant for those interested in AI and cybersecurity, as it addresses critical security concerns associated with autonomous AI agents.

**「Impact on Ordinary Life」** OpenShell&\#x27;s introduction of a secure, private runtime for autonomous AI agents could lead to more trust in AI applications by ensuring that these agents operate within defined boundaries, protecting sensitive data and systems. This could have implications for everyday life, such as in smart homes or in the workplace, where AI agents handle sensitive tasks and information. It raises the question of how we can balance the benefits of AI automation with the need for robust security measures.
