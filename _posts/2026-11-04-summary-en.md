This tool is designed to help gardeners adapt to the challenges posed by climate change. By evaluating various factors such as annual average temperature, natural distribution, winter cold, and climate dryness, GCAT predicts which plants can survive in future conditions. The tool is based on a climate assessment model initially launched in Australia and aims to assist gardeners in making informed decisions about their plant choices, ultimately fostering climate-resilient gardens.
