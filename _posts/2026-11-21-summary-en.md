rss · 美联储 FEDS Notes 经济研究解读 · Nov 20, 19:30

**「Understanding the Impact of AI on Productivity and Interest Rates」** The Federal Reserve&\#x27;s analysis explores the relationship between productivity growth and interest rates, particularly in the context of AI advancements. This research examines how productivity booms, often driven by technological innovations like AI, can influence long-term interest rates. The study uses empirical data and vector autoregressive \(VAR\) models to analyze the impact of total factor productivity \(TFP\) growth on interest rates. It finds that contrary to standard economic theory, a persistent increase in TFP growth, which is anticipated by the public, can lead to declines in both the 10-year Treasury yield and the expected real short rate. This suggests that the recent rise in interest rates may not be solely due to anticipated higher productivity growth. The research also considers the role of AI in shaping productivity expectations and its implications for the economy.

**「Impact on Financial Decisions」** The analysis suggests that a surge in productivity, especially driven by technological advancements like AI, may not necessarily lead to higher interest rates as traditionally expected. This could have significant implications for financial planning and investment strategies. For instance, if long-term interest rates remain low despite productivity gains, it might be more favorable for borrowing and investment in capital-intensive projects. Conversely, it raises the question of how to assess the true cost of capital in an era where productivity and technological advancements are reshaping economic landscapes.
