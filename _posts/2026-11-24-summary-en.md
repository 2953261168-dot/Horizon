rss · 美联储 FEDS 经济金融工作论文 · Nov 23, 16:35

**「Understanding the Decline of Bank Branches in the U.S.」** The study &\#x27;Where&\#x27;s The Bank? Banking Access in the Era of Branch Consolidation&\#x27; by Robert M. Adams and Shane M. Sherlund, published by the Federal Reserve, examines the significant decline in the number of bank branches in the United States between 2014 and 2024. This period saw a reduction of over 19 log points in the number of bank branches, raising concerns about the accessibility of banking services. The authors utilize a spatial competition model and demographic data to analyze this trend. The research highlights that the contraction in bank branches is consistent with the equilibrium response of a competitive spatial system to an industry-wide profitability shock, rather than a localized withdrawal of service.
